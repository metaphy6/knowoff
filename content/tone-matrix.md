# Knowoff Tone Matrix

Every media asset in a Knowoff pack is tagged into one of four humor
buckets. The buckets keep pack mixes balanced and give curators a shared
vocabulary for why something fits.

| Bucket | Vibe | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **millennial-cope** | Nostalgia, anxiety, earnest absurdity, "adulting" fatigue. | A 90s cartoon frame about debt; a coffee meme about burnout. |
| **gen-z-absurdism** | Surreal, non-sequitur, irony-poisoned, TikTok logic. | A cursed stock photo with no caption; a bread loaf in a dramatic scene. |
| **social-awkwardness** | Cringe, misread signals, second-hand embarrassment. | A text exchange that went wrong; a party scene where everyone is standing wrong. |
| **chaos** | No thesis, just vibes; the wild card that breaks pattern-matching. | Random shapes, explosive GIFs, nonsense text. |

## Curator rules

- Every Nown and card must carry exactly one bucket tag.
- A pack should aim for a roughly even mix across buckets; no bucket should
  dominate a release.
- "Chaos" is a spice, not a meal: keep it under ~30% of a pack.
- Erotic-leaning media is allowed only in age-gated packs and must still map
  to one of the four buckets (usually millennial-cope or chaos).
