# Knowoff Tone Matrix

Every Nown and card in a Knowoff text pack is tagged into one of four humor
buckets. The buckets keep pack mixes balanced and give curators a shared
vocabulary for why something fits.

Apply these buckets across the five text modes under the
[lo-fi text direction](../BLUEPRINT.md#playable-media-direction), adopted
2026-09-12. They describe humor, not mode eligibility, action correctness,
rendering polish or draw probabilities. The themes below are editorial
illustrations, not approved candidates or production-pool evidence.

| Bucket | Vibe | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **millennial-cope** | Nostalgia, anxiety, earnest absurdity, "adulting" fatigue. | Nostalgic expectations colliding with bills, work or minor adult responsibilities. |
| **gen-z-absurdism** | Surreal, non-sequitur, irony-poisoned, TikTok logic. | Overconfident non-sequiturs and incongruous everyday items with several possible readings. |
| **social-awkwardness** | Cringe, misread signals, second-hand embarrassment. | Misread invitations, awkward silences and plausible responses to social mistakes. |
| **chaos** | No thesis, just vibes; the wild card that breaks pattern-matching. | Unexpected item choices and deliberately strange responses that still leave room for a defense. |

## Curator rules

- Every Nown and card must carry exactly one bucket tag.
- A pack should aim for a roughly even mix across buckets; no bucket should
  dominate a release.
- "Chaos" is a spice, not a meal: keep it under ~30% of a pack.
- Erotic-leaning text is allowed only in age-gated packs and must still map
  to one of the four buckets (usually millennial-cope or chaos).

## Tone, editorial dimensions and relevance

Tone describes the text's style. Separately, record its human situation, comic
mechanism, cultural reach, shelf life and accessibility in editorial planning
records, using [Humor development](humor-development.md). A card can be
`social-awkwardness`, use awkward honesty about work, and be evergreen; these
dimensions do not create new buckets or runtime metadata fields.

The experimental **70% evergreen / 20% seasonal or cultural / 10% topical**
release mix is a starting point for playtests, not a fixed quota. Assess it
independently of the tone mix and revise it using player feedback and reuse
data. Neither editorial mix sets card draw probabilities or per-hand quotas.

The relevance mesh's **chaos band** is also separate from the `chaos` tone
bucket: the band measures a card's relationship to a particular Nown; the
bucket describes humor. Shared tone, tags, characters or callbacks do not
establish a relevance band. For optional embedding-based relevance evidence,
record the evaluator/version and measured relationship. Always test reviewed suitability and legal actions
in real 4- and 6-player schedules for each mode, as described in the
[Curator guide](curator-guide.md). Cosine similarity alone cannot certify a
rating, replacement, trade or comparison chain.
