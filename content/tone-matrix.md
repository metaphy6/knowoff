# Knowoff Tone Matrix

Every media asset in a Knowoff pack is tagged into one of four humor
buckets. The buckets keep pack mixes balanced and give curators a shared
vocabulary for why something fits.

Apply these buckets across the [lo-fi image/text media direction](../BLUEPRINT.md#playable-media-direction).
They describe humor, not rendering polish or media-type shares. A static image
or text example below is not a format quota.

| Bucket | Vibe | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **millennial-cope** | Nostalgia, anxiety, earnest absurdity, "adulting" fatigue. | A rough snapshot of someone slumped beside a cold coffee; a 90s cartoon frame about debt. |
| **gen-z-absurdism** | Surreal, non-sequitur, irony-poisoned, TikTok logic. | An ordinary gesture frozen at the wrong moment; a cursed stock photo with no caption. |
| **social-awkwardness** | Cringe, misread signals, second-hand embarrassment. | An awkwardly cropped unanswered wave; a text exchange that went wrong. |
| **chaos** | No thesis, just vibes; the wild card that breaks pattern-matching. | Random shapes, incongruous objects, nonsense text. |

## Curator rules

- Every Nown and card must carry exactly one bucket tag.
- A pack should aim for a roughly even mix across buckets; no bucket should
  dominate a release.
- "Chaos" is a spice, not a meal: keep it under ~30% of a pack.
- Erotic-leaning media is allowed only in age-gated packs and must still map
  to one of the four buckets (usually millennial-cope or chaos).

## Tone, editorial dimensions and relevance

Tone describes the asset's style. Separately, record its human situation, comic
mechanism, cultural reach, shelf life and accessibility in editorial planning
records, using [Humor development](humor-development.md). A card can be
`social-awkwardness`, use awkward honesty about work, and be evergreen; these
dimensions do not create new buckets or runtime metadata fields.

The experimental **70% evergreen / 20% seasonal or cultural / 10% topical**
release mix is a starting point for playtests, not a fixed quota. Assess it
independently of the tone mix and revise it using player feedback and reuse
data. Neither editorial mix sets card draw probabilities or per-hand quotas.

The relevance mesh's **chaos band** is also separate from the `chaos` tone
bucket: the band measures a card's relationship to a particular Nown; the
bucket describes humor. Shared tone, tags, characters or callbacks do not
establish a relevance band. Test that relationship in the mesh and in real
4- and 6-player hands, as described in the [Curator guide](curator-guide.md).
