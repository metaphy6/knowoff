## Brief and source choices

Provisional audience: general, non-erotic; English (`en`) review labels, everyday work, social awkwardness and domestic mishaps. All premises are evergreen. Regional recognition and translations are untested. The explicit 6/4 request replaces a full pilot's larger language/theme scope. Tone is recorded once per candidate, separately from format and semantic relevance.

The earlier `openart-samples-20260911` attempt recorded ten concepts in the local checkpoint, with two source generations but no completed local assets. On 2026-09-11 OpenArt reported zero remaining credits; the earlier source download failed with HTTP 403. This batch uses actual archival film motion for samples 01–06 and the built-in image generator for samples 07–10. It does not purchase credits or claim the planned Astra production pipeline ran. Previous generation attempts and unrelated batches are preserved.

The loops are excerpts from staged silent comedies, not authentic candid phone captures or newly generated footage. Their rough photographic texture, subject actions and abrupt resets are deliberate archival interpretations of the lo-fi brief. Recurring performers may cause fatigue; this is a source/style experiment for the owner's review. See [loop provenance](sources/loop-provenance.json) for exact source pages, attributions, excerpts and rights limitations.

The four stills adapt the previous sample-07–10 concepts: overpreparation, misplaced courtesy, incongruous importance and unexplained aftermath. They are fictional AI-generated photos. The image tool did not expose its model version or seed; [the actual prompts and generation provenance](sources/still-provenance.json) state that limitation. Sample 08 received a targeted posture revision to make the person defer visibly to the bag. Original PNGs remain in the image tool's output directory; the review deliverables are compressed WebPs.
