## App name and short description

**Knowoff** — **Bluff, argue, vote.**

## Core description draft

Some players know the secret. Others are making it up.

Knowoff is a text-based social deduction party game for exactly 4 or 6 players.
Nowers see a short secret situation, plan or criterion. Donowers read the table
and try to blend in. Choose a card, defend your choice and vote. Catch every
Donower before the Knowoff votes run out — or bluff your way to a team win.

Join a compatible Quick Play table online or invite friends to a Local Room
with a code or QR link. Guests can join through a browser. Local Rooms are
free and uncapped. Turn actions use cards from your hand; you do not have to
write a story or improvise a timed speech.

The intended five-mode offering is listed below. **Include only enabled modes
in the published listing**; do not publish “five playable modes” while some are
still gated. The release operator replaces this editorial instruction with
tested availability before store submission.

| Mode | One-line player action |
|---|---|
| Missed the Briefing | Play a response to a conversation you may not understand. |
| Secret Scale | Place an item on a scale whose meaning some players cannot see. |
| Make Room | Swap one item into a shared three-item bag. |
| Bad Bargains | Offer a card trade and accept or refuse another player's offer. |
| Top That | Play an item you can argue tops the previous one. |

Discuss through Quick Chat, reactions and moderated free text, or speak with
friends around the table. Knowoff ballots are live: see who votes for whom,
and change your vote before the window closes. All modes use the same roles
and team victory rules. No purchase changes roles, dealing, votes or scoring.

## Economy copy — include only after production integration passes

Earn Noin through eligible play and spend it on Play Passes, cosmetics and text
theme packs. Gameplay modes have no separate unlock charge. Free Quick Play
allowances and daily reward limits are shared across modes. A Noin Play Pass
provides unlimited Quick Play while active; it does not remove ads. Premium
is a cash subscription providing ad removal and unlimited Quick Play while
active. Bad Bargains trades fictional game cards, never currency or purchases.

Subscription billing periods, actual local prices, renewal/cancellation terms,
refund/restore flow and any rewarded-ad claims must match tested platform
products and the approved disclosures. Do not imply Premium is earnable with
Noin or that unverified receipt/ad integrations are live.
