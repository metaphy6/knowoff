## App name

Knowoff

## Short description (30 chars)

Bluff, guess, vote.

## Full description

Knowoff is a social deduction party game for exactly 4 or 6 players. Every
round one media item — a static image or text — appears on every phone except
the Donowers'. Donowers must fake it. Nowers must spot the lie. After
accusations, pokes, and Quick Chat jabs, everyone votes. Catch every Donower
before the votes run out.

Play Quick Play online with strangers or gather in a Local Room with a QR
code. Earn Noin by playing well, spend it on Play Passes, cosmetics, and
theme packs — or subscribe to Premium for ad-free unlimited play. Nothing you
buy affects dealing, roles, votes, or scoring.

## Key bullets

- One Nown, one lie: can you spot who can’t see it?
- Quick Play matchmaking for 4 or 6 players.
- Local Rooms with QR codes — no install required for guests.
- Canned Quick Chat + reactions: accuse, defend, react.
- Earn Noin every match; spend it or subscribe to Premium.
- Weekly leaderboard and community challenges.

## Privacy notice (first launch)

Knowoff uses an anonymous device account by default. Link Google or Facebook
to carry progress across devices. We store only your provider subject id and
email; your public profile is pseudonymous. See the full privacy policy at
<https://play.knowoff.example/privacy>.

## Premium subscription disclosure

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription that removes ads and grants
unlimited Quick Play while active. Prices are shown in-app. Manage or cancel
in your platform account settings.
